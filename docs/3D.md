# Stylized 2D/3D look (main)

Experimental look on top of `style/hd`. Rules, physics and data are identical to `main`; only rendering changed.
This look is now the main version (site root). Title screen shows `v0.8`. There is no real 3D engine: the same camera
projection is kept, and the depth is faked by shading (so it stays light and runs like the HD version).

- Arena (`src/art/hd/arenaHd.ts`): court as a raised slab (front and side faces, contact shadow), ambient occlusion along
  the walls, cast glass shadows, specular sheen, thick glass with streaks and lit rails, cylindrical posts, glossy LED
  ad board, dusk arena with a bokeh crowd and warm spotlight cones.
- Venue (`src/art/hd/venueHd.ts`, v0.153): stepped stands with a seated crowd on three sides (softened by down/up-sampling), LED
  barrier boards, run-off carpet, courtside people and props; a cheering variant of the crowd is faded in after points.
- Characters (`characterHd.ts`): volume shading (key light upper left, core shadow lower right), cool rim light, specular dots.
- Ball: sphere shading, specular highlight, rim light. UI: extruded rounded buttons with a gradient face.
- Real 3D (three.js, 3D court and rigged players) would be a separate, bigger step; see BACKLOG.
