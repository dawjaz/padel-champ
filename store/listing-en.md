**Full description** (max 4000)

Every point is a decision. PorTres is a padel game where you call the shots: when your pair has the ball, you pick the shot and where it goes. Your stats, your stamina, the walls and the positions of all four players decide the rest.

CALL EVERY SHOT
• Over 20 real padel shots: bandeja, víbora, bajada, volley, block, chiquita, lob, rulo, contrapared, drop shot and more.
• Smash the ball out of the court with a Por Tres or a Por Cuatro.
• Play the glass: balls come off the back and side walls, and the mesh fence bounces them anywhere.
• Tap the spot where the ball should land. The court shows you where your rivals can reach, and where the gaps are.
• Touch the timing ring on smashes, viboras, bandejas and serves: a clean hit is safer and stronger.

UNLEASH SPECIAL SHOTS
• Four special shots, each with its own cut-in: Sky Rocket, Venom, Corner Lock and Freeze.
• Strong defenders can still get one back, if they have the guts left.

BUILD YOUR CAREER
• Create your player, choose your side of the court and pick a partner from ten.
• 24-week seasons across 24 cities, from the Club Cup to the Season Finals.
• Climb the world ranking, beat your rivals and fill your trophy cabinet.
• Level up, spend skill points and collect 120 pieces of gear from six brands. Gear changes your look and raises your stats.

TRAIN AT CAMP
• Four training games: target practice, serves, timing and the Smash Show.
• Earn stars, coins and gear, and complete daily and weekly goals.

FEEL THE MATCH
• A TV-broadcast court with comic big-head players.
• A crowd that rises after the big points, and arenas in six styles across the cities.
• Short animated lessons teach you the game, and you can skip them at any time.

Plays offline. No account needed.
