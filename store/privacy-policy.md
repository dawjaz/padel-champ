## In short
The game does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no
in-app purchase in the current version, and nothing about you or your device is sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the game stores, and where
Your progress and settings (your player, career, gear, trophies, statistics, sound and other options) are saved
**only on your device**, in the app's local storage (in a browser: the site's local storage). They never leave your
device. You can delete them at any time with Settings › RESET SAVE, by uninstalling the app or, in a browser, by
clearing the site's data.

## Network
The installed app works offline. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages: when you open it, your browser downloads the
game's files from GitHub's servers, and GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address for security and
operation, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
We do not receive those records.

## Permissions
The game asks for no permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or files).

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

## Third parties
The game includes no third-party services or software development kits that collect data. Its fonts are bundled
with the game and are not loaded from other servers.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR and similar laws you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data held about you. We hold none, but you
are welcome to contact us at the address above with any question.

## Changes
If a future version adds features that handle personal data (for example an online ranking, advertising or purchases),
this policy will be updated **before** that version is released, and the game will ask for your consent where the law
requires it. The effective date above shows when the policy last changed.
